**Q13**
- **①** Correct. Under 第24条第１項, the transferor must notify every transferee, and there is no exception when the transferee is a 貸金業者.
- **②** Correct. 第24条第２項 applies 第17条 (except 第６項) to the transferee, so Ｂ must deliver the document.
- **③ (the answer)** Correct.
  - **The rule:** 第24条第３項 forbids the transfer when Ａ "知り、若しくは知ることができるとき" that a 取立て制限者 will later receive the claim. 暴力団員等 falls under 第１号 of that definition.
  - **Punishment route:** 第24条の6の4第１項第２号 explicitly excludes 第24条第３項 and 第４項 from general 法令違反, so the explanation is right to point to the dedicated 第３号 and 第４号.
  - **Nuance:** 第４号 applies only when both イ (Ａ cannot prove it had good reason not to know) and ロ (the 取立て制限者 violates 第21条第１項 or commits a crime) are met. The explanation says "対象となり得る", and it spells out both conditions, so it is accurate.
- **④** Correct. The 親族 of an individual 貸金業者 counts as 密接な関係 under 施行令第3条の7第１号. Under 第24条の6の4第１項第９号, the 貸金業者 is punished only if it cannot prove it took 相当の注意, so whether Ａ took care matters.

**Q16**
- **①** Correct. 第10条第１項第１号: the heir files within 30 days of learning of the death.
- **② (the answer)** Correct. Under 第１項第２号, the person who files is "その法人を代表する役員であつた者", meaning the officer of the dissolved company Ａ, within 30 days of the merger.
- **③** Correct. Under 第１項第３号, the 破産管財人 files, and under 第２項 the registration lapses.
- **④** Correct. The closure of business is 第１項第５号 and the lapse is 第２項. 第43条 covers "第十条第二項の規定により登録が効力を失つたとき" and treats the former lender as a 貸金業者 only within the scope of 取引を結了する目的.

**Q17**
